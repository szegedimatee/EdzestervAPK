# Edzésterv

A 50-week triathlon training plan leading to two half-distance races, and the athlete's log of what was actually done. The Hungarian word in brackets is the one the plan data and the app UI use.

## Plan

**Plan** (_edzésterv_):
The fixed, day-by-day schedule of 350 Workouts, one per day, starting on a set date.
_Avoid_: Schedule, program

**Week** (_hét_):
Seven consecutive days of the Plan, numbered 1–50 from the Plan's start.

**Block** (_blokk_):
Five consecutive Weeks with one shared training focus; the Plan has ten.
_Avoid_: Phase, cycle, mesocycle

**Workout** (_edzés_):
The single planned session for one day: its Sport, planned duration, planned distance, Zone and description.
_Avoid_: Session, training, item

**Today** (_mai nap_):
The Workout whose day matches the current calendar date.

## Sports

**Sport** (_sportág_):
The discipline a Workout prescribes: Swim, Bike, Run, Brick, Shakeout, Race or Rest Day.

**Swim** (_úszás_):
A swimming Workout.

**Bike** (_kerékpár_, _bringa_):
A cycling Workout, ridden outdoors or on a Trainer. When the Plan says _görgő/bringa_, the athlete picks either.
_Avoid_: Cycle, ride

**Trainer** (_görgő_):
A stationary indoor bike setup; a Bike Workout on it is a trainer ride.
_Avoid_: Turbo, rollers, indoor bike

**Run** (_futás_):
A running Workout.

**Brick** (_tégla_):
A Workout of Bike immediately followed by Run, trained as one session.
_Avoid_: Combo, double

**Shakeout** (_átmozgatás_):
The short, easy swim + bike + run on the day before a Race, used to rehearse transitions.
_Avoid_: Activation, opener, warm-up

**Rest Day** (_pihenő_):
A day with no training load prescribed.

## Races

**Race** (_verseny_):
A half-distance triathlon (1.9 km swim, 90 km bike, 21.1 km run) that the Plan targets; there are two, each with an athlete-editable name and date.
_Avoid_: Event, competition

**Half-distance** (_féltáv_):
The 70.3 triathlon distance.
_Avoid_: Half Ironman, middle distance

**A-race** (_A-verseny_):
The Race the whole Plan peaks for: the second one, at Balatonfűzfő. The first Race is a lower-stakes tune-up.

**Taper** (_tapering_):
The reduced-load final part of a Block before a Race.

## Effort

**Zone** (_zóna_):
The planned intensity band of a Workout, Z1 (easiest) to Z3; a range like Z1-Z2 spans two bands.

**RPE**:
The athlete's own 1–10 rating of how hard a Workout felt.
_Avoid_: Effort score, feel

## Log

**Log** (_napló_):
Everything the athlete records against the Plan, one Log Entry per Workout at most.
_Avoid_: Journal, history, diary

**Log Entry**:
What the athlete actually did for one Workout: measured distances per Sport, pace or speed, average heart rate, RPE or notes, and Fueling.
_Avoid_: Result, record, actuals

**Done** (_kész_):
A Workout whose Log Entry has a measured distance above zero. Every Rest Day also counts as Done.

**Skipped** (_kihagyott_):
A past, non-Rest-Day Workout without a Done Log Entry.
_Avoid_: Missed, failed

## Fueling and gear

**Fueling** (_frissítés_):
Carbohydrate and fluid taken in during a Workout or Race.
_Avoid_: Nutrition, feeding

**CH** (_szénhidrát_):
Carbohydrate, counted in grams; Fueling targets are grams of CH per hour.
_Avoid_: Carbs

**Gear Wear**:
The distance a shoe or bike chain has covered since it was last Reset, compared against its replacement limit.

**Reset** (_nullázás_):
Marking a new shoe or chain as fitted, so its Gear Wear counts from zero.
